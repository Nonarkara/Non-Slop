31 flood apps, labelled by eye

18 Oct 2026. Crimson is the slop group. Ink is the clean group. A longer bar is not always the worse one: the clean map is the long bar.

Forty-six citizen flood apps were listed on FloodDash. Thirty-seven responded, and thirty-one were labelled. Median shadows were 1 on the clean apps and 14 on the slop apps. Median line length was 26em against 61em. Precision 0.95 and recall 0.91 are in-sample and optimistic. The same check sent 2 of 5 professional map apps to REVIEW.
